## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

---

### 💰 Is there any hidden fee in the Pro plan?
No. You are charged based on the **number of active users** in your account.

### 💳 Do I need a credit card to try?
Free plan does not require a credit card.  
Paid plans require payment details during checkout.

### 🧩 Is there any limitation in the free version?
Yes. Free plan has limited screenshot frequency and historical data retention.

### 🔁 How to change the credit card?
You can update payment details from the Billing dashboard or through the payment provider portal.
